- Buy boxes are effected by:
	- Offer > Properties
	- Sold Listings > Properties
- This relationship is used at real time in the [[Quote Engine]] to influence the quote

Two simple rules:
- **Rule 1 — Per buyer** A buyer's quotes drift their own buy box range slowly over time (when calculating real time)
- Rule 2 — If system generated buy box, buyer data (in aggregate) will shift correct the starting range for everyone in that area. This also includes [[Comps System (Separate from Buyers)]]
	- Example: If we have 10 system generated buy boxes for Dilworth (a neighborhood in North Carolina) and most buyers in an area are consistently quoting way off the starting range, we start to shift the buybox range
