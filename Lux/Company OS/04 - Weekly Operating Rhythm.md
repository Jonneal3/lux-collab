# Weekly Operating Rhythm

Daily rhythm
- Morning: review new leads, buyer replies, urgent market changes, and open blockers
- Midday: acquisitions, dispo, quoting, and priority calls
- End of day: update key notes, record outcomes, and move next actions forward

Weekly cadence
- Monday: leadership priorities and market review
- Tuesday: acquisitions and seller pipeline review
- Wednesday: product, tech, and automation review
- Thursday: marketing and buyer development review
- Friday: scorecard review, misses, wins, and next-week planning

Weekly required outputs
- One founder summary
- Updated market priorities
- Updated backlog
- Updated department scorecards
- At least one process or automation improvement shipped or documented
