# Weekly Operating Rhythm

Purpose
- Keep Lux moving with a repeatable cadence that humans and bots can both support.

Daily rhythm
- Morning: review new leads, hot properties, urgent buyer replies, and open blockers
- Midday: acquisitions and dispo execution, follow-up, and quoting
- End of day: log updates, move tasks, and capture what changed

Weekly leadership cadence
- Monday: market review, top priorities, blocker clearing
- Tuesday: sales and acquisitions pipeline review
- Wednesday: tech and automation build review
- Thursday: marketing performance and campaign adjustments
- Friday: wins, misses, scorecard review, and next-week prep

Weekly outputs
- One founder summary note
- Updated department scorecards
- Updated market priorities
- One automation or SOP improvement shipped

Monthly outputs
- Active market ranking review
- Expansion market review
- Budget and unit economics review
- Tool and automation stack review

Non-negotiables
- Every department updates one note each week
- Every market that matters has a current owner
- Every recurring bottleneck gets logged for automation or SOP work
