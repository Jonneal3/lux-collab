# 12 Month Goals

Goal 1
- Become operationally dominant in a small set of luxury infill neighborhoods before broad expansion.

Goal 2
- Build a buyer intelligence system that captures real buy boxes, buyer behavior, pricing appetite, and feedback by market.

Goal 3
- Build a pricing and quote engine that materially reduces guesswork and improves speed to actionable offers.

Goal 4
- Turn founder knowledge into reusable playbooks, SOPs, and automation-ready notes.

Goal 5
- Build the internal software and AI workflows required to automate repetitive research, note synthesis, buyer matching, and reporting.

Success markers
- Strong buyer coverage in every active market
- Faster lead-to-quote time
- Higher quote confidence
- Repeatable market-entry process
- Less founder memory dependency in day-to-day execution
