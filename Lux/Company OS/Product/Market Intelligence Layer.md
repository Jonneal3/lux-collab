# Market Intelligence Layer

Purpose
- Show where Lux should focus attention by combining buyer demand, builder activity, pricing behavior, and market velocity.

Signals to track
- Density of luxury end-buyer demand
- Density of buy boxes in an area
- Speed of relevant transactions
- Strength of teardown and new-construction comp activity
- Buyer quote clustering and consistency
- Areas transacting at high rates
- Areas with many premium new-build exits
- Areas where buyer demand is strong but coverage is still thin

Ideal outputs
- Neighborhood heat maps
- Market priority rankings
- Flags for hot pockets inside larger metros
- Better decisions on where to market and where to spend founder attention

Strategic use
- Help decide which neighborhoods deserve direct mail, buyer research, and founder attention first
- Help decide where Lux should tighten or expand active market coverage
