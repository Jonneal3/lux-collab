# Market Expansion Engine

Goal
- Make adding a new market a repeatable system rather than an ad hoc research project.

Workflow
1. Pick a narrow set of luxury infill neighborhoods
2. Gather teardown comps and new-build exit signals
3. Identify active builders, developers, and repeat luxury operators
4. Seed buyer records and starting system buy boxes
5. Test outreach, quoting, and market response
6. Keep the market narrow until confidence is real

What the future system should automate
- Company and builder discovery
- Site and listing scraping for target neighborhoods and pricing cues
- Buyer import and enrichment
- Test-property outreach to learn buy boxes and quote ranges
- Starting market scorecards and neighborhood heat maps
