# ARV and Exit Demand Signals

Purpose
- Add finish-line pricing and resale-demand context into market and quote logic.

Signals to track
- Recently sold new construction in the last year
- High-value new construction listings currently on market
- How many premium new builds exist in the area
- Days on market and price reductions for relevant luxury product

Why it matters
- Lux is not only buying dirt; Lux is underwriting the buyer's likely exit environment.
- Strong ARV and absorption signal helps explain why certain dirt values are sustainable.
