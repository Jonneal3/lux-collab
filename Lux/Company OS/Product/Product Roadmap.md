# Product Roadmap

Product mission
- Build an internal operating platform that helps Lux source, understand, price, and move luxury infill opportunities faster than a manually run business can.

Current product pillars
- Buyer intelligence
- Quote engine and pricing confidence
- Market expansion and seeding
- Data quality and workflow automation
- Parcel-level intelligence

Phase 1
- Stabilize teardown comp quality
- Improve quote confidence
- Standardize buyer and buy box data capture
- Keep active market setup repeatable

Phase 2
- Pull buyer signals from calls, texts, emails, and documents
- Route properties into pricing and buyer matching faster
- Improve market seeding and builder discovery workflows
- Build better admin and review views for operators

Phase 3
- Self-improving market intelligence from quote outcomes, comp patterns, and buyer behavior
- Better parcel scoring and neighborhood scoring
- Better predictive buyer routing
- Reporting loops that require minimal manual effort
