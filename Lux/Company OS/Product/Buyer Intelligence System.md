# Buyer Intelligence System

Goal
- Turn raw buyer interactions into structured acquisition intelligence.

Input sources
- Emails
- Phone calls
- SMS
- Map screenshots
- ZIP lists
- Website research
- Publicly visible project and listing history
- Historic project and development activity
- Current for-sale inventory on buyer or builder websites

Collection modes
- Formally inbounded information from direct buyer communication
- Publicly scraped information from websites and listing trails
- Operator-entered notes from calls, meetings, and ad hoc market feedback

What the system should capture
- Target neighborhoods
- Excluded neighborhoods
- Teardown versus renovation preferences
- Lot size expectations
- Per-lot or per-square-foot price preferences
- Per-property budget limits
- Busy-road tolerance and other edge-case preferences
- Actual quotes and non-response signals
- Product-type preferences such as residential or commercial
- Build mode such as spec, custom, investor, developer, or mixed
- Build-style preferences and edge-case notes
- Current and prior project references when available

Important rule
- Passive signals matter too. Silence, vague no-thanks replies, and selective engagement all help define the real buy box.

Outputs
- Buyer-specific buy boxes
- Confidence-weighted buyer profiles
- Better buyer matching
- Better price expectations for new opportunities

Near-term product direction
- Convert calls, texts, emails, and uploaded documents into structured buy box drafts faster
- Connect buyer quotes and outcomes back into property records automatically
- Use repeated buyer behavior to improve future routing and pricing confidence
