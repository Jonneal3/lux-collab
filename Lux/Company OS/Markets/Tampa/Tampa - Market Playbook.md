# Tampa - Market Playbook

Why Lux cares
- South Tampa contains premium infill neighborhoods where lot scarcity and established luxury demand can make teardown opportunities very attractive.

Focus neighborhoods
- Davis Islands
- Beach Park
- Palma Ceia
- Sunset Park

Best buyer types
- Experienced South Tampa spec builders
- Flood-aware operators with efficient plan sets and permitting familiarity
- Buyers comfortable with larger lot checks in premium school and lifestyle pockets

What makes a strong deal here
- Large clean lot in a known infill pocket
- New-construction comp support nearby
- Easy story on why the finished product belongs in the neighborhood

Main risks
- Flood-zone complications
- Insurance and elevation costs
- Overpaying for land if build constraints are brushed aside
