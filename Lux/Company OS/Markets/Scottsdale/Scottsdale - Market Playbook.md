# Scottsdale - Market Playbook

Why Lux cares
- Scottsdale and adjacent Paradise Valley offer concentrated luxury demand with highly legible premium neighborhoods and strong appetite for luxury redevelopment.

Focus neighborhoods
- Paradise Valley
- Silverleaf / DC Ranch
- Arcadia
- Troon / Desert Mountain

Best buyer types
- Luxury custom and semi-spec builders
- High-end desert-market operators
- Buyers who understand product-positioning differences across Scottsdale submarkets

What makes a strong deal here
- Premium neighborhood identity
- Clear luxury resale market
- Buyers with enough capital to handle higher lot checks and longer execution windows

Main risks
- Product fit and finish expectations can be very high
- Buyer appetite can vary meaningfully by sub-pocket even inside the same broad market
