# West Palm Beach - Market Playbook

Why Lux cares
- West Palm Beach has multiple neighborhood pockets where teardown and historic-sensitive redevelopment create real scarcity and strong builder demand.
- The best opportunities are not broad WPB. They are very specific blocks and submarkets where buyers already know how to build or restore.

Focus neighborhoods
- El Cid
- Flamingo Park
- South End / SoSo
- Old Northwood

Best buyer types
- Luxury spec builders
- Historic-sensitive redevelopment groups
- Repeat local operators with Palm Beach County permitting experience
- Developers with strong design and entitlement partners

What makes a strong deal here
- Near-water or neighborhood-core location
- Large lot relative to surrounding housing stock
- Clear teardown or premium redevelopment angle
- If historic, a buyer who knows how to work inside that constraint

Main risks
- Historic district restrictions
- Buyer pool narrows fast if the parcel is specialized
- Pricing can look attractive on paper but be wrong if build constraints are ignored

Current priorities
- Keep a strong builder map for El Cid, Flamingo Park, and SoSo
- Separate historic-sensitive buyers from generic teardown buyers
- Keep live notes on active projects and local players
