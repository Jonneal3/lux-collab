# Austin - Market Playbook

Why Lux cares
- Austin has premium neighborhoods with strong end-buyer pricing, constrained desirable lots, and well-capitalized buyers who understand luxury infill product.

Focus neighborhoods
- Westlake Hills
- Tarrytown
- Rollingwood
- Barton Hills / Barton Creek
- Old Enfield / Pemberton Heights
- Lake Austin Waterfront

Best buyer types
- Luxury spec builders
- Design-driven custom and semi-spec operators
- Capitalized buyers focused on premium dirt in legacy neighborhoods

What makes a strong deal here
- Premium school, water, or legacy-neighborhood story
- Tight land supply
- Strong new-construction exit evidence

Main risks
- A lot may be expensive simply because it is scarce, not because it is an easy build
- Highly nuanced micro-location differences matter a lot in Austin
