# Charlotte - Market Playbook

Why Lux cares
- Charlotte has several established luxury infill neighborhoods where teardown scarcity and strong new-build pricing create a cleaner builder market than many smaller cities.

Focus neighborhoods
- Eastover
- Dilworth
- Foxcroft
- Sedgefield
- Myers Park
- Cotswold
- SouthPark

Best buyer types
- Luxury spec builders
- Infill-focused developers
- Buyers already active in Myers Park-adjacent and South Charlotte neighborhoods

What makes a strong deal here
- Established luxury neighborhood identity
- Strong new-build comp support
- Limited nearby available land
- Easy access to buyers who already know the submarket

Main risks
- Pricing gets noisy fast when a property is merely adjacent to the best areas rather than truly inside them
- Pocket-level understanding matters more than city-level labels
