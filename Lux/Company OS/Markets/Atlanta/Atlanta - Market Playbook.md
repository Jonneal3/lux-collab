# Atlanta - Market Playbook

Why Lux cares
- Atlanta offers deep capital, strong buyer depth, and premium neighborhoods where developers and aggressive luxury operators can stretch harder than ordinary builders.

Focus neighborhoods
- Buckhead
- Tuxedo Park
- Chastain

Best buyer types
- Luxury spec builders
- Developers and investor-builders with strong capital bases
- Repeat operators already active in Buckhead submarkets

What makes a strong deal here
- Prime Buckhead-area location
- Strong new-build comp support
- Buyer pool deep enough to create real bidding tension

Main risks
- High buyer sophistication means weakly packaged deals will get exposed quickly
- Large-market noise can create false positives if the neighborhood focus is too broad
