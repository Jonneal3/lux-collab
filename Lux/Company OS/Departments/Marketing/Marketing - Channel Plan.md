# Marketing Channel Plan

Seller-side channels
- Direct mail into teardown and luxury infill neighborhoods
- Cold SMS and call follow-up once list quality is strong
- Probate attorneys
- Estate sale companies
- Divorce and relocation referrals
- Contractor and local trades referral network
- Digital retargeting for warm inbound traffic

Buyer-side channels
- Apollo and targeted list building
- Builder and developer database growth by market
- Email updates on new opportunities
- Case-study style market notes that prove neighborhood knowledge

Message strategy
- Speak to speed, certainty, discretion, and local knowledge
- Emphasize off-market convenience and straightforward execution
- For buyers, emphasize fit, deal clarity, and repeat flow

Content engine ideas
- Weekly market pulse by neighborhood
- Tear-down lot economics snapshots
- Buyer spotlight notes
- Before and after pricing logic on real deals

Rules
- Every campaign should map to a market and neighborhood thesis.
- No broad generic luxury messaging without a local angle.
- Marketing should feed acquisitions and sales, not operate in isolation.
