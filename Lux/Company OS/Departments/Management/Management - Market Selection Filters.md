# Management - Market Selection Filters

Primary market filters
- Focus on the hottest neighborhoods, not broad city coverage
- Prefer markets where developers and well-capitalized investors will outbid ordinary builders
- Prefer markets where luxury end-buyer demand is already proven by new construction exits
- Accept that tighter spreads can still work if buyer depth and turnover are strong enough

What management should validate before committing harder to a market
- Builder and developer density
- Real teardown comp quality
- Luxury new-build absorption
- Reliable buyer feedback loops
- Neighborhood-level clarity on what is desirable and what is not
