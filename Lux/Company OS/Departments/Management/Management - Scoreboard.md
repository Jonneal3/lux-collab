# Management Scoreboard

North star
- Build a repeatable machine that can enter, dominate, and automate high-spread luxury infill markets.

Weekly scoreboard
- Seller pipeline: new leads, qualified conversations, quotes sent, contracts signed
- Buyer pipeline: new buyers added, buyers qualified, active buy boxes captured, bids received
- Market health: markets with fresh teardown comps, neighborhoods with strong buyer coverage, stale markets needing review
- Financial health: gross assignment revenue, expected revenue pipeline, marketing spend, software spend
- Execution health: automations shipped, SOPs added, major blockers open, hours saved

Review questions
- Which market improved this week?
- Which market is noisy but not productive?
- Which workflow is still too dependent on founder memory?
- What can be delegated to software, bots, or an operator next?
