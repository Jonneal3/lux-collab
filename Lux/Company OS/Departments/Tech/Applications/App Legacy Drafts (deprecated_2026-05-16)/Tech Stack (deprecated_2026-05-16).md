Layers (what you draw on mapbox)
- These are things like:
	- Parcels
		- Source: County GIS (ArcGIS)
		- What it is: property boundaries
		- What it tells you: exact lot you are analyzing
	- Flood Zones
		- Source: FEMA
		- What it is: flood risk maps
		- What it tells you: flood risk + insurance risk
	- Slope
		- Source: USGS elevation data
		- What it is: terrain height data
		- What it tells you: flat vs steep land (build cost impact)
	 - Zoning
		- Source: City / County ArcGIS
		- What it is: land use rules
		- What it tells you: what you are legally allowed to build
		- Important Sub Items:
			- use: mixed, residential, commercial etc.
			- zoned: eg. r1, r2 
			- height limits: max building height allowed
			- frontage: road access requirements
	- Historic
		- Source: NRHP / local city GIS
		- What it is: protected areas
		- What it tells you: restrictions on building or changes
