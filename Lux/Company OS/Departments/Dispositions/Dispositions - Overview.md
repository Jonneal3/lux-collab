# Dispositions Overview

Mission
- Move each deal to the strongest-fit buyer at the best executable price in the shortest reasonable time.

Current priorities
- Improve buyer match quality
- Increase bid tension
- Standardize deal packaging
- Capture buyer feedback fast enough to improve the next quote

Core modules
- Buyer matching
- Deal packaging
- Sendout workflow
- Offer comparison
- Buyer feedback loop
- Closing coordination

KPIs to watch
- Time from contract to buyer sendout
- Number of qualified buyers reached per deal
- Offer rate per sendout
- Spread between initial expectation and best offer
- Close rate by buyer segment

AI support
- Buyer matching assistant
- Sendout draft generator
- Offer comparison summary
- Buyer objection tracker
