# Finance Overview

Mission
- Track unit economics, cash needs, and return on effort so Lux can scale the right markets and workflows.

Current priorities
- Understand revenue by market and channel
- Track marketing spend against contracts and revenue
- Separate fixed tool spend from growth spend
- Build a simple operating model for expansion decisions

Core modules
- Revenue tracking
- Channel economics
- Tool and software spend
- Budget planning
- Market-level profitability
- Expansion hurdle rates

KPIs to watch
- Gross assignment revenue by market
- Revenue per contract
- Cost per qualified lead
- Cost per contract by source
- Monthly software and data spend
- Cash required to expand one additional market

AI support
- Weekly finance recap
- Market profitability summary
- Spend categorization support
- Simple forecast drafting
