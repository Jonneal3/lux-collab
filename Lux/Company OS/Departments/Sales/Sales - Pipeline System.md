# Sales Pipeline System

Pipeline stages
- Prospect: potential buyer identified
- Contacted: first outreach sent
- Qualified: real operator with clear asset appetite
- Buy box captured: target neighborhoods, lot size, price range, build type, and preferences logged
- Active: buyer ready for real inventory
- Bidding: buyer has an active look at a deal
- Closed: buyer completed a transaction with Lux
- Dormant: buyer exists but needs reactivation or cleanup

Required buyer fields
- Market and neighborhoods
- Build type: spec, custom, developer, investor, or mixed
- Preferred lot size and home size
- Price range and target margin
- Preferred product style
- Notes from calls, texts, and prior offers

Rules
- No buyer is considered active without a usable buy box.
- Every serious buyer conversation should update the record the same day.
- Buyer feedback should inform pricing and market confidence.
- Dormant buyers should either be reactivated or deprioritized, not ignored.

Automations to add
- Extract buy box notes from communications
- Draft follow-up after each meaningful buyer touch
- Alert when a buyer has gone stale
- Match new opportunities to likely buyers automatically
