# Sales - Buyer Sourcing Methods

Core buyer sourcing channels
- Redfin method using recent and local builder activity, sometimes paired with permit research
- LinkedIn lookalikes
- Apollo list building
- Direct call, email, and text outreach
- Google Maps nearby project and office research
- Facebook research
- Agent and broker referrals

Ideal buyer targets
- Luxury spec builders
- Buyers who repeatedly buy in the same neighborhoods
- Deep-pocketed operators who can handle larger lot prices
- Developers and investors who will stretch harder than ordinary builders in the best markets

Rule
- Buyer sourcing should create structured profiles, not just names in a list.
