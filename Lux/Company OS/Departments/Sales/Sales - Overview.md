# Sales Overview

Mission
- Build buyer relationships, move opportunities to the right buyers fast, and improve bid tension on every deal.

Current priorities
- Clean up the buyer database by market
- Capture and maintain real buy boxes
- Package deals clearly and quickly
- Track buyer response quality and speed
- Build repeatable follow-up loops for active and dormant buyers

Core modules
- Buyer sourcing
- Buyer qualification
- Buy box capture and maintenance
- Deal sendouts
- Follow-up sequences
- Buyer feedback loop into pricing

KPIs to watch
- New qualified buyers per week
- Buyers with active buy boxes
- Deals sent per active property
- Buyer reply rate
- Offer rate
- Average time from sendout to first serious reply

AI support
- Call summary to CRM note
- Buy box extractor
- Buyer profile summarizer
- Follow-up draft generator
