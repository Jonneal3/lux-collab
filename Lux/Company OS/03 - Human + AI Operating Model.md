# Human + AI Operating Model

Purpose
- Build one operating layer where founders, operators, bots, and coding agents can collaborate without losing context.

Human ownership
- Scott Little: relationships, buyer nuance, seller nuance, strategy judgment, high-leverage decisions
- Jon Neal: product, systems, data, automation, internal tooling, operating design
- Shared: market focus, underwriting standards, and company direction

AI ownership
- Turn raw notes into structured docs
- Draft and maintain market playbooks
- Summarize calls, texts, emails, and meetings
- Keep backlog, updates, and scorecards organized
- Research buyers, builders, neighborhoods, and comps
- Help design and document internal software

Working rules
- One canonical note per topic
- Decisions should live near the system they affect
- Drafts are allowed, but the long-term goal is structured notes over scratchpads
- Bots can draft and synthesize; humans approve strategy and irreversible decisions
- Every important workflow should eventually become a note, a template, an SOP, or an automation

Recommended update format
- Date
- Owner
- Topic
- What changed
- Why it matters
- Blockers
- Next action
