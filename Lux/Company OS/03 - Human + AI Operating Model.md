# Human + AI Operating Model

Purpose
- This vault should let founders, operators, and AI agents work from the same playbook.

Human ownership
- Scott Little: relationships, market judgment, buyer and seller nuance, capital and strategic decisions
- Jon Neal: systems, product, data, automation, process design, tooling
- Shared: final priorities, market selection, and deal quality bar

AI ownership
- Draft notes from calls, texts, emails, and meetings
- Turn raw ideas into structured plans, SOPs, and scorecards
- Research target markets, buyers, builders, and competitive patterns
- Maintain backlog notes, update logs, and draft weekly summaries
- Help build and document internal software and automations

Working rules
- One note per system or topic, not one giant scratch pad.
- Important decisions should be logged in the relevant department note.
- Every meaningful update should include date, owner, change, blocker, and next step.
- Bots can draft, organize, and summarize. Humans approve strategy and irreversible decisions.
- Keep market notes neighborhood-specific whenever possible.

Recommended note statuses
- Draft
- Active
- Blocked
- Waiting
- Archived

Recommended update format
- Date:
- Owner:
- Department:
- What changed:
- Why it matters:
- Blockers:
- Next action:

Best use cases for agents
- Build new SOPs from founder voice notes
- Update department scorecards from CRM and app outputs
- Draft buyer summaries and market briefs
- Turn feature ideas into product specs
- Keep this vault structured as the business grows

Guardrails
- This vault is for Lux Ventures Group only.
- Keep sensitive data organized, not scattered.
- Use one source of truth for each workflow.
