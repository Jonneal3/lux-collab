# Buy Boxes System

## Overview

The platform supports two types of buy boxes:

1. System Buy Boxes
2. Buyer Buy Boxes

These are used by the [[Quote Engine]] to:
- Match buyers to deals
- Estimate buyer interest
- Improve pricing confidence

For more:
- [[Initial Buy Boxes]]
- [[Buy Boxes - Evolving Through Time]]

---

# System Buy Boxes

System buy boxes are:
- Automatically created
- Shared across the platform
- Used as default starting templates

They represent:
- Market assumptions
- Area intelligence
- Typical buyer behavior

Examples:
- Atlanta Luxury Builder Default
- Dilworth Luxury Infill Default

A buyer will automatically inherit a system buy box as their starting acquisition profile.

---

# Buyer Buy Boxes

Buyer buy boxes represent:
- Actual buyer preferences
- Explicit acquisition criteria
- Real-world buying behavior

These may come from:

## Inbound Methods
- Emails
- SMS
- Phone calls

And may arrive in multiple formats:
- Plain English text
- Map screenshots
- ZIP lists

## Research
- Website research
    - Many companies have current/past listings and historic projects on their websites
- Redfin
    - Historic projects
    - Prior development activity

Buyer buy boxes:
- Override system buy boxes
- Are higher confidence
- Are buyer-specific

Examples:
- Specific neighborhoods
- Lot size requirements
- Teardown-only strategies
- Excluded areas
- Price limitations
- Busy road tolerance

---

# Relationship Between the Two

A buyer may:
- Use a system buy box as a starting template
- Have a completely custom buyer buy box

All buyers must have an active buy box at all times, whether:
- System-generated
- Buyer-specific

System buy boxes act as:
- Starting intelligence
- Market defaults

Buyer buy boxes act as:
- Real acquisition criteria
- Buyer-specific overrides
